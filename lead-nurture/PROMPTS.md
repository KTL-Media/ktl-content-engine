# PROMPTS — lead-nurture

## 1. Process prompt

> Act as a world-class operations consultant. I want to document my process for
> LEAD NURTURE — turning captured leads into booked clients with a short
> follow-up sequence — so an AI agent can execute it exactly the way I do. The
> sequence: Day 0 welcome with immediate value, Day 2 proof, Day 5 soft offer
> (reply MAP), Day 9 last touch, then stop. Any reply pauses automation and
> comes to me same day; unsubscribes honored immediately. I approve every
> message before anything activates. Interview me ONE question at a time. Ask
> about: what each touch says and why; email vs DM per touch; how I segment
> leads; what counts as a hot reply I handle personally; what my offer actually
> is (don't invent pricing); what "done right" looks like; the mistakes a
> beginner always makes (too many touches, hard closes, ignoring replies,
> invented claims). Keep asking until you can write the full process back as a
> numbered SOP. Ask your first question now.

## 2. Toolbox prompt

> Build the TOOLBOX for my lead-nurture playbook. It must contain: the four
> draft message templates (Day 0 welcome, Day 2 proof, Day 5 offer, Day 9 last
> touch) clearly marked DRAFT — I approve exact wording before activation; the
> offer one-pager with pricing marked TBD — never invent it; the send log
> format; the rules (max 4 touches, replies pause automation, hot replies to me
> same day, unsubscribe honored same day); and the segmentation (email-captured
> gets the full sequence, kit-only gets DM touches only). Keep it tight.

## 3. Proof prompt

> Build the PROOF checklist for my lead-nurture playbook, run before activation
> and per batch once live. Items: every message matches my approved copy
> character-for-character; correct segment; max 4 touches then stop; reply
> handling armed; unsubscribes honored and logged; every send logged; no
> invented pricing, claims, or results. Final item is the approval gate: no
> sequence activated and no message sent until I approved the exact copy of
> every message and the exact segment rules. End with a version label and the
> rule: only activate work that passes 100%.

## 4. Correction prompt

> My lead-nurture playbook got something wrong. Don't re-prompt me in the chat
> — fix the playbook files instead. Diagnose first: was PROCESS.md unclear
> (sequence, reply handling, segmentation)? Was TOOLBOX.md missing a template
> or rule? Was PROOF.md too weak to catch the failure — especially the
> activation gate? Then edit the right file and tell me in one line what
> changed and which future runs it fixes. If it's a judgment call I need to
> make, ask me one direct question instead of guessing.
