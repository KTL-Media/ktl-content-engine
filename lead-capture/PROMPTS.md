# PROMPTS — lead-capture

## 1. Process prompt

> Act as a world-class operations consultant. I want to document my process for
> LEAD CAPTURE — turning Instagram comments into tagged email leads via a
> ManyChat keyword flow — so an AI agent can execute it exactly the way I do.
> The flow: comment with keyword PLAYBOOK → public reply → DM #1 delivers the
> starter-kit PDF → DM #2 asks for email → tag and log the lead → hand to
> nurture. NOTHING activates without my approval of the exact DM copy, keyword,
> and post. Interview me ONE question at a time. Ask about: what triggers the
> flow (per-post vs account-wide keywords); my public reply style; what goes in
> DM #1 vs DM #2; how I ask for the email without being pushy; my tag taxonomy;
> what a lead record contains; what "done right" looks like; the mistakes a
> beginner always makes (wrong copy live, keyword armed everywhere, emails not
> logged). Keep asking until you can write the full process back as a numbered
> SOP. Ask your first question now.

## 2. Toolbox prompt

> Build the TOOLBOX for my lead-capture playbook. It must contain: the ManyChat
> account (themart125th@gmail.com) and its current status; the three draft DM
> copy blocks (public reply, DM #1 delivery with PDF link, DM #2 email ask)
> clearly marked DRAFT — I approve exact wording before activation; the keyword
> config (PLAYBOOK, case-insensitive, per-post arming, self-comment exclusion);
> the tag taxonomy (playbook-lead, email-captured, replied-setup with the hot
> flag rule); the lead record format; the starter-kit PDF path; and the metrics
> I report weekly. Keep it tight, no fluff.

## 3. Proof prompt

> Build the PROOF checklist for my lead-capture playbook, run before any
> activation and weekly once live. Items: correct ManyChat account; keyword
> armed only on approved posts; every DM matches my approved copy
> character-for-character; kit link resolves; email capture logs with source;
> tags applied; dummy-comment test run passed. Final item is the approval gate:
> the flow was NOT activated, published, or tested on a live post until I
> approved the exact DM copy, exact keyword, and exact post. End with a version
> label and the rule: only activate work that passes 100%.

## 4. Correction prompt

> My lead-capture playbook got something wrong. Don't re-prompt me in the chat
> — fix the playbook files instead. Diagnose first: was PROCESS.md unclear
> (flow steps, arming rules)? Was TOOLBOX.md missing copy, a tag, or the
> account status? Was PROOF.md too weak to catch the failure — especially the
> activation gate? Then edit the right file and tell me in one line what
> changed and which future runs it fixes. If it's a judgment call I need to
> make, ask me one direct question instead of guessing.
